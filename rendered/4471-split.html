The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. A tenant whose locale writes the day first gets its dates flipped. This is what #4471 is about, and the queued fix covers it. It is the large majority of the reports, reported through the app, email and the form on booking-v2.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type MM/DD/YYYY. Three tenants run that form (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour) and all three write the day first. Their people type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. Only reports that came through the form on those tenants belong here. A parser fix does not change what the hint tells people to type. Fixing the parser alone will leave these reports open.

clock: the invite moves by one day, not by a flipped month. The reports describe a weekday that is off by one (Tuesday became Monday), an invite that arrived before the booking was made, and the same meeting landing on different days for Lisbon and Toronto. That points at time zones or a date boundary, not at day and month order. The desk already marked these reports as “read as a one-day move” in desk-notes.json, and filed them under parser anyway. The cause is still a guess: this ticket needs an engineer to look.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges on the subject line alone. Subjects here are generic (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Date is wrong”), so all three problems share them. Intake does not help either: its tag guesses from the reporter’s browser and not from the tenant, so it disagrees with the cause for a large part of the reports. The cause depends on the tenant (the locale it writes dates in, and the booking form it runs), as causes.ts already does.

The export also repeats some reports: its pages overlap, so a report can come twice. The counts here use one row per id.

What to do

  • Keep #4471 for parser and let the queued fix close it (ticket 1).
  • hint has its own ticket (ticket 2), and so does clock (ticket 3).
  • Count reports per cause, not per subject, when you merge the next big incident.
